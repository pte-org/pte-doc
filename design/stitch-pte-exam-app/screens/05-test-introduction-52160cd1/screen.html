Test Introduction

The Pearson Test of English Academic (PTE Academic) assesses your English language ability in speaking, writing, reading, and listening across academic contexts. The test is divided into three timed parts, plus an optional scheduled break.

Part Content Time allowed
Part 1 Speaking & Writing (includes Personal Introduction, Read Aloud, Repeat Sentence, Describe Image, Re-tell Lecture, Answer Short Question, Summarize Written Text, and Write Essay) 54 – 67 minutes
Part 2 Reading (includes Fill in the Blanks, Multiple Choice Multiple Answers, Re-order Paragraphs, and Multiple Choice Single Answer) 29 – 30 minutes
Optional break Scheduled rest break (Candidate may raise hand to leave testing room) 10 minutes
Part 3 Listening (includes Summarize Spoken Text, Multiple Choice, Fill in the Blanks, Highlight Correct Summary, Select Missing Word, Highlight Incorrect Words, and Write from Dictation) 30 – 43 minutes
