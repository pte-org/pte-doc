Critical Examination Rules

Mandatory Protocol
play_disabled

1. Single Audio Playback (No Pauses)

Each audio recording plays once only. Playback begins automatically after a short countdown (between 3 and 12 seconds). You cannot pause, restart, or rewind the recording at any time.

schedule

2. Independent vs Pooled Timing Allocation

Summarize Spoken Text operates on an individual 10-minute countdown per question. All subsequent listening tasks share a single pooled timer (~20–30 minutes). Budget approximately 1 to 1.5 minutes per remaining item.

volume_up

3. Master Headset Volume Control

Keep your headset over both ears. Master audio volume can be adjusted anytime during playback using the speaker control icon located in the upper-right corner of the global header.

edit_note

4. Erasable Noteboard & Marker Booklet

Use your erasable booklet to note key nouns, figures, and sequential arguments while listening. Verify your fine-point marker is capped when not writing to prevent ink dry-out.

warning

5. Negative Marking Penalty Warning

Incorrect answers in Multiple Choice, Multiple Answer and Highlight Incorrect Words incur a 1-point deduction each. If you are uncertain of an extra selection, do not guess. Minimum score per task is 0.
