# Rubric readiness matrix v1

| Canonical task | Rubric family | Required reference/evidence | Initial status | Blocker before publish |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `PERSONAL_INTRODUCTION` | Coaching-only | Prompt and optional recording | Limited | Must remain unscored and excluded from scored feedback. |
| `READ_ALOUD` | Speaking content/audio | Prompt text, response audio, approved assessor for pronunciation/fluency | Partial | Audio assessor integration required for audio criteria; text-only feedback may use content coverage only. |
| `REPEAT_SENTENCE` | Speaking content/audio | Source audio/transcript, response audio/transcript | Not ready | Source and response alignment plus audio evidence. |
| `DESCRIBE_IMAGE` | Speaking content/audio | Pinned image, approved annotations, response evidence | Not ready | Image reference and anti-invention evidence policy. |
| `RE_TELL_LECTURE` | Speaking content/audio | Lecture source/transcript, response evidence | Not ready | Source provenance and coverage policy. |
| `ANSWER_SHORT_QUESTION` | Objective/speaking | Accepted answers/variants, response evidence | Not ready | Accepted variant and short-answer evaluator policy. |
| `RESPOND_TO_A_SITUATION` | Speaking content | Situation requirements, response transcript/audio | Not ready | Fulfilment and register descriptors. |
| `SUMMARIZE_GROUP_DISCUSSION` | Speaking content/audio | Speaker/source evidence, response evidence | Not ready | Speaker attribution and relationship policy. |
| `SUMMARIZE_WRITTEN_TEXT` | Writing summary | Passage, response, form policy | Ready for draft | Academic anchors and approved source references. |
| `WRITE_ESSAY` | Writing | Prompt, response, form policy | Ready for draft | Academic anchors and criterion calibration. |
| `MC_READING_SINGLE` | Objective | Pinned passage, option IDs, answer key | Ready for draft | Evaluator/reference provenance. |
| `MC_READING_MULTIPLE` | Objective | Passage, option set, partial-credit policy | Ready for draft | Set/penalty policy and examples. |
| `RE_ORDER_PARAGRAPHS` | Objective | Opaque block IDs, accepted order policy | Ready for draft | No answer-order leakage in student projection. |
| `FILL_IN_THE_BLANKS_DRAG_AND_DROP` | Objective | Blank IDs, word bank, reuse policy | Ready for draft | Per-blank evaluator and reference provenance. |
| `FILL_IN_THE_BLANKS_DROPDOWN` | Objective | Blank groups and option IDs | Ready for draft | Dynamic group evaluator. |
| `SUMMARIZE_SPOKEN_TEXT` | Writing summary | Audio, lecture transcript, response, form policy | Not ready | Source transcript and evidence retention. |
| `MC_LISTENING_SINGLE` | Objective | Audio/transcript, option IDs, answer key | Not ready | Audio/source revision. |
| `MC_LISTENING_MULTIPLE` | Objective | Audio/transcript, option set, partial-credit policy | Not ready | Audio/source revision. |
| `FILL_IN_THE_BLANKS_TYPE_IN` | Objective | Audio, passage, blank IDs, normalization policy | Not ready | Accepted spelling/normalization version. |
| `HIGHLIGHT_CORRECT_SUMMARY` | Objective | Audio/source, summary option IDs | Not ready | Source-grounded explanation policy. |
| `SELECT_MISSING_WORD` | Objective | Audio/source, candidate endings | Not ready | Context evidence and answer key. |
| `HIGHLIGHT_INCORRECT_WORDS` | Objective | Audio/source, opaque token IDs | Not ready | Token alignment and penalty policy. |
| `WRITE_FROM_DICTATION` | Objective | Audio/source, typed response, normalization policy | Not ready | Alignment and accepted-word policy. |

`Ready for draft` means the current task family has enough domain shape to create a draft. It does not mean a revision may be published. Every row still needs approved references, anchors and runtime compatibility.
