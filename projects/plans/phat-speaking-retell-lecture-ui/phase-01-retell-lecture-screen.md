# Phase 1: Strings + `RetellLectureScreen`

## Requirements
A working `RetellLectureScreen` widget: fixed 2-variable instruction text (interpolating the 10-second pre-record sub-stage and the 40-second response window, not the combined 70-second prep total), an always-visible `AudioListeningStatusCard` that runs its own "Beginning in 3 seconds" → "Playing" sequence (same shape as `RepeatSentenceScreen`'s), and an always-visible `RecordedAnswerStatusCard` that runs its own independent "Beginning in" → "Recording" → upload-status sequence — fully automatic recording and advance, structurally identical to `RepeatSentenceScreen`.

## Steps
1. Add three new strings to `lib/features/exam_attempt/speaking_writing/constants/speaking_writing_strings.dart`: `retellLectureInstructionPrefix = 'You will hear a lecture. After listening to the lecture, in '`, `retellLectureInstructionMiddle = ' seconds, please speak into the microphone and retell what you just heard from the lecture in your own words. You will have '`, `retellLectureInstructionSuffix = ' seconds to give your response.'`. Do not duplicate any of the already-shared strings (`recordingCurrentStatusLabel`, `recordingBeginningInPrefix`/`Suffix`, `audioListeningPlayingPrefix`/`Suffix`, `audioListeningVolumeLabel`) — reuse them as-is.
2. Create `lib/features/exam_attempt/speaking_writing/presentation/pages/retell_lecture_screen.dart`: `RetellLectureScreen extends StatefulWidget` with the exact same constructor shape as `RepeatSentenceScreen` (`task`, `attemptPublicId`, `recorder`, `mediaDao`, `coordinator`, `syncEngine`). `_RetellLectureScreenState` uses `with AutoRecordTimerBridgeMixin<RetellLectureScreen>`, constructs an `AutoRecordCubit` in `initState` and calls `startAutoRecordBridge(...)`, closes the cubit and calls `disposeAutoRecordBridge()` in `dispose`, and mirrors `RepeatSentenceScreen`'s `build` exactly (`BlocProvider.value` + `ExamScaffold` + `AutoAdvanceOnUploadReady<AutoRecordCubit, AutoRecordState>` as `bottomAction`).
3. Define `const int _preListenSeconds = 3;` and `const int _preRecordSeconds = 10;` at the top of the file, with a doc comment explaining these differ from `RepeatSentenceScreen`'s `3`/`3` only in the pre-record duration: the "Beginning in 3 seconds" delay before the lecture audio starts is the same as Repeat Sentence's, but the pre-record "chuẩn bị" window is 10 seconds (not 3). Audio-playing duration is `task.prepSeconds - _preListenSeconds - _preRecordSeconds` (57 when `prepSeconds = 70`).
4. Build the body (`_RetellLectureBody`, mirroring `_RepeatSentenceBody`'s exact structure): `InstructionText` built from `'${SpeakingWritingStrings.retellLectureInstructionPrefix}$_preRecordSeconds${SpeakingWritingStrings.retellLectureInstructionMiddle}${task.responseSeconds}${SpeakingWritingStrings.retellLectureInstructionSuffix}'` — interpolating the local `_preRecordSeconds` constant (10) and `task.responseSeconds` (40), **not** `task.prepSeconds` (70); this is the single easiest mistake in this phase, called out explicitly here and must be double-checked against the literal mockup wording before moving on. Followed by `_ListeningCard(task: task, snapshot: snapshot)` and `_RecordCard(task: task, recordingState: state, snapshot: snapshot)`, same `BlocSelector<ExamAttemptBloc, ExamAttemptState, TimerSnapshot?>` + `BlocBuilder<AutoRecordCubit, AutoRecordState>` wiring as `RepeatSentenceScreen`.
5. Port `_elapsedPrepSeconds`, `_ListeningCard`, and `_RecordCard` (including its `_countdownLabel`/`_elapsedFraction`/`_uploadStatusLabel` helpers) verbatim from `repeat_sentence_screen.dart`, changing only the module-level `_preListenSeconds`/`_preRecordSeconds` constants they reference — a pure constant swap, since `_preListenSeconds = 3` keeps the branch logic's behavior identical in shape to `RepeatSentenceScreen`'s (no unreachable-branch case here). Do not alter the branching logic itself.
6. Run `flutter analyze` on the new file and confirm zero unresolved-reference or unused-code warnings.

## Success Criteria
- `flutter analyze` is clean for the new `retell_lecture_screen.dart` and the edited `speaking_writing_strings.dart`.
- `grep -n "Text('" lib/features/exam_attempt/speaking_writing/presentation/pages/retell_lecture_screen.dart` returns no matches (no hardcoded `Text('literal')`, per `docs/CODING_STANDARDS_APP.md`).
- Manually tracing the instruction-text computation for `prepSeconds: 70, responseSeconds: 40` yields exactly: "You will hear a lecture. After listening to the lecture, in 10 seconds, please speak into the microphone and retell what you just heard from the lecture in your own words. You will have 40 seconds to give your response."
- Manually tracing `_ListeningCard` at `elapsed = 0` (start of prep) yields "Beginning in 3 seconds"; at `elapsed = 3` it yields "Playing 57 seconds left".

## Risks
- Interpolating `task.prepSeconds` instead of `_preRecordSeconds` into the instruction template: Mitigation — Step 4 explicitly calls this out as the primary hazard; verified by manual trace in Success Criteria before Phase 3's automated test confirms it.
- Accidentally changing `_ListeningCard`/`_RecordCard`'s branching logic while porting (rather than only its referenced constants): Mitigation — Step 5 requires verbatim porting, diffed mentally against `repeat_sentence_screen.dart` before moving to Step 6.
- Forgetting to close `AutoRecordCubit`/call `disposeAutoRecordBridge()` in `dispose`, leaking a subscription: Mitigation — Step 2 states the exact lifecycle mirror explicitly; `flutter analyze` and Phase 3's widget tests would surface a leaked stream/timer if this were missed.
