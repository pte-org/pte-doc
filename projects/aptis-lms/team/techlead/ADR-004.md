# ADR-004: Transactional outbox and idempotent BullMQ workers
**Date:** 2026-06-20
**Status:** Accepted

## Context
Submission, scoring, notifications and analytics must survive process crashes and at-least-once queue delivery without losing events or duplicating external effects.

## Decision
We will persist domain state and an outbox record in one PostgreSQL transaction. A relay publishes versioned jobs to BullMQ; consumers require stable idempotency keys and persist processing outcomes.

## Consequences
### Positive
- No dual-write gap between database commits and queue publication.
- Safe retries and independent Worker scaling.
- Event contracts create future service-extraction seams.

### Negative / Trade-offs
- Requires outbox cleanup, monitoring and replay tooling.
- Events are eventually consistent outside the originating transaction.

### Risks
- Queue or relay backlog; monitor oldest-outbox age and dead-letter counts with exam-hour alerts.
